Mapping Tartary

European maps of Tartary, from the TARTARY Project collection, in a deep-zoom viewer. The first reading edition, Ortelius's map of 1570, shows where this is going: every text on the map and on the back of the sheet, in Latin as engraved and in English, opened from a numbered pin.

The collection

    About this site

    The collection's catalogue and images come from the TARTARY Project's first site, tartary.vercel.app, built by Alexia Tsang. This page reads a copy of that catalogue taken on 29 Oct 2026 and opens the same deep-zoom images.

    The Ortelius texts were transcribed and translated by an AI model, Claude Opus 5.5, reading Internet Archive's scan of the Boston Public Library copy, then scored against Marcel van den Broecke's edition at orteliusmaps.com. They are a test of the method and have not yet been reviewed by a specialist. The Ricci and Miller maps are feature demonstrations from September 2026, with illustrative texts.
