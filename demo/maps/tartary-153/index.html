Map of the very curious and most remarkable voyages accomplished throughout Asia, Tartary, Mangi, Japan, the East Indies, adjacent islands, and Africa. Begun in the year 1252. By Marco Polo, the Venetian, by Polo and van der Aa, 1735. Image: e-rara. One of the 137 maps of Tartary in the TARTARY Project's collection at HKUST, catalogued on tartary.vercel.app. Its texts have not been transcribed yet.
